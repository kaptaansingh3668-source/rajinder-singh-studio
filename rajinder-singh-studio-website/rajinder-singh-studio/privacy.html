Privacy notice.

This starter privacy notice must be reviewed and completed for your actual business before the website goes live. It is not legal advice.

Information you provide

If you submit a project enquiry, the information you enter may include your name, email address, project location, budget range and project description. Only collect information needed to respond to your enquiry.

How information is used

Enquiry information should be used to respond to your request, assess project fit and communicate about the proposed services. If you connect a third-party form provider, review that provider's privacy terms and configure it appropriately.

Retention and your rights

Set a sensible retention period, protect enquiry data, and provide a genuine contact method for privacy questions and rights requests. Your final notice should identify the real data controller and any relevant processors.

Cookies and third-party services

This starter page does not add an analytics or advertising pixel. If you add analytics, advertising, embedded media or other non-essential tracking, assess the applicable UK privacy and cookie requirements before enabling it.
